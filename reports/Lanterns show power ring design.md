# Lanterns turns the power ring into equipment

On screen, the power ring in *Lanterns* (Home Box Office (HBO) and HBO Max, with DC Studios, where DC originally stood for Detective Comics; eight episodes, 16 August to 4 October 2026) is a **chunky, signet-style ring**. It has a **thick, dark, cracked, rock-textured band** and a round face with the Lantern emblem **outlined in gold around a green gem or crystal**. It is muted in colour and visibly battle-worn, built to look like "a tool and real equipment" rather than an alien jewel. John Stewart's interview ring in Episode 3 is a separate, cleaner prop: a **black band with silver trim** and a **concentric face** (black centre dot, then green, then a further ring, then trim). The prop master has confirmed that the two rings differ. The story mostly follows one ring. Hal wears it, is buried with it, and John takes it from his finger in Episode 6 and wears it through the finale. Next to the comics and the 2011 film, the show keeps the band-plus-gem-face layout but makes it **not all green**, swaps the glossy alien-artifact look for **age as character**, and uses glowing effects sparingly. How the ring was made is almost entirely unpublished. No source names a designer or fabricator, gives materials or measurements, names the finger or hand, or says whether the glow was lit on set. For a Minecraft modeller, the parts worth copying are general principles rather than the logo: a face wider than the band, a dark band that never lights up against a bright layered face, glow kept to the face, and wear or trim metal as the way to tell variants apart.

## A cracked dark band frames a gold-rimmed green gem

The fullest description of Hal Jordan's ring comes from the first official image, shown around **12 December 2025**. The band is "thick and dark", rock-like and **cracked along its length**, so the ring looks thousands of years old. The face shows the classic emblem **outlined in gold with a green gem at its centre** ([The Direct, 8 Jan 2026](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-james-gunn-dcu-ring); [ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/?p=2103803)). An April 2026 official look adds a "**more crystallized**" centre built from **concentric rings** ([The Direct, 14 Apr 2026](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-ring-dc-live-action)). The teaser shows the ring "suggesting years of battles" ([SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/news/654056-lanterns-teaser-green-lantern-ring-third-john-stewart)), and press notes its "**noticeably muted colors**" ([ComicsBeat](https://www.comicsbeat.com/lanterns-hbo-max-2026-preview/)). No source mentions prongs or an open setting. Together, the descriptions point to a **flat-topped signet with an inset or slightly raised translucent face**, not a cut stone held up off the band. The translucency is inferred from the words "gem" and "crystallized". No source confirms the prop's material.

The sources do not settle how the ring sits on the hand. Recaps say only that the ring goes "on his finger". John "pries it off Hal's fingers" in Episode 5 and takes it from the buried Hal's finger in Episode 6. **No source names the finger or hand for Kyle Chandler or Aaron Pierre** ([TVLine Ep 1](https://www.tvline.com/2237785/lanterns-premiere-recap-season-1-episode-1-hbo/); [The Ringer Ep 5](https://www.theringer.com/2026/09/13/dc/lanterns-episode-5-lights-out-recap-hal-jordan-john-stewart-manhunter); [TVLine Ep 6](https://www.tvline.com/2263270/lanterns-season-1-episode-6-guy-gardner-nathan-fillion/)). Band width, face diameter, side profile and height off the finger are also unpublished. Checking frames of the episodes is the only way to fill these gaps.

### One ring, two wearers, and a second prop for the rookie

**Hal and John are not two Lanterns with two rings for the whole series.** The plot moves a single ring between them. John puts it on in an Episode 1 flashback before a car crash. In Episode 5 he takes Hal's ring and lantern and then refuses to be a Lantern. In Episode 6 Guy Gardner says the ring has been decommissioned on Oa, then brings it, and John takes it from the buried Hal's finger ([TVLine Ep 1](https://www.tvline.com/2237785/lanterns-premiere-recap-season-1-episode-1-hbo/); [MEAWW](https://meaww.com/does-john-stewart-become-green-lantern-lanterns-episode-5-lights-out-ending-explained-recap-hbo-max-ring-cast-release-date-reddit); [TVLine Ep 6](https://www.tvline.com/2263270/lanterns-season-1-episode-6-guy-gardner-nathan-fillion/)). The visibly different ring is the one the Guardians hand John during his Episode 3 interview. A fan comparison of stills described its silver trim and layered black-and-green face, and prop master **Jeffrey Johnson** replied "Indeed he did" ([The Direct, 27 Sep 2026](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring)). The only difference anyone reports in how the two men wear the ring is **condition**. In the first production photo Hal is "scuffed" and John "absolutely clean" ([Sandler's Handlings](https://sandlershandlings.substack.com/p/max-comes-bearing-first-lanterns-photo-to-mark-production-start)).

This corrects an earlier claim that Guy receives a "newly forged ring" in Episode 6 ([Nerdist](https://nerdist.com/article/why-doesnt-guy-gardner-have-hal-jordan-ring-lanterns/)). Later recaps do not back it up, and Tom King's "Guy has the ring" comment points the other way. Treat it as unconfirmed ([TVLine](https://www.tvline.com/2238858/green-lantern-hal-jordan-ring-lanterns-explained-guy-gardner/)). Two other early reports conflict with later evidence. The February 2025 photo read Hal's ring as "golden with a green head", but the later close-up shows a dark band with gold only on the emblem outline. The likely explanation is gold trim seen from a distance. An April piece gave John gold trim, but September episode stills show silver, which the prop master's reply supports ([The Direct, 14 Apr](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-ring-dc-live-action) vs [27 Sep](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring)). In both cases the later, closer evidence wins.

## Green light is rationed and lives in the effects, not the band

The show treats ring power as scarce. Recaps say the ring "shines" just before Hal's hologram appears. Hal puts a green bubble around an alien suspect before it explodes, and the alien tells him the ring needs recharging at the lantern ([TVLine Ep 1](https://www.tvline.com/2237785/lanterns-premiere-recap-season-1-episode-1-hbo/)). The constructs are small: a bottle opener, fake money, a watch John is asked to make as a test, and a bird he makes when he tries to fly. Hal goes to full power once. Reviewers contrast this with the "garish" 2011 film ([Looper](https://www.looper.com/2240128/lanterns-avoid-worst-mistake-green-lantern-movie/); [Gizmodo Ep 3](https://gizmodo.com/lanterns-season-1-episode-3-outkast-recap-review-2000803095)). Charge works like a meter. Orbital strikes drain Hal's ring to about **5%**, and John later spends that last 5% to summon the "Halogram" of Hal ([The Ringer](https://www.theringer.com/2026/09/13/dc/lanterns-episode-5-lights-out-recap-hal-jordan-john-stewart-manhunter); [TVLine Ep 6](https://www.tvline.com/2263270/lanterns-season-1-episode-6-guy-gardner-nathan-fillion/)). Recharging is a physical act: a promo still shows a fist pressing the ring against a "strange, alien-looking" battery, the only saturated colour in a drab frame ([Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/tv/lanterns-promo-image-has-actual-green-in-it/)). Showrunner Chris Mundy said the team "wanted to earn the times we used it in a big way" ([OtakuKart, citing IGN](https://otakukart.com/lanterns-showrunner-explains-why-episode-2s-big-green-lantern-ring-moment-had-to-be-earned/)).

In the finale, Sinestro forges an unsanctioned lantern with "a bit of yellow… a bit of fear" in it. John charges Hal's old ring on it, recites a new oath ("I choose this ring. I choose this fight.") and flies off ([Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/?p=1030191); [Gizmodo Ep 8](https://gizmodo.com/lanterns-season-1-episode-8-dirt-stars-recap-review-2000821475)). No source describes the ring changing colour afterwards, and no yellow Sinestro ring appears in season 1 ([Looper](https://www.looper.com/2240373/lanterns-episode-2-ulrich-thomsen-sinestro-debut-explained/)). Nothing published covers whether the gem glows at rest, the exact green, or any flare or particle treatment.

## A "real equipment" brief, but the ring's makers stay anonymous

Mundy gave the clearest statement of intent: "We wanted everything in the show to feel like a tool and real equipment." That meant visible wear without taking away "all the green". Costume designer **Cynthia Ann Summers** said the noir world shaped her choices, and the co-creators wanted the suit, ring and battery to feel **tactile rather than fantastical** ([SYFY Wire](https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/why-lanterns-broke-tradition-with-green-lantern-hal-jordan-suit-exclusive)). Summers picked the suit's green from "a whole color chart of greens". The suit went through about 20 concepts, avoided a "futuristic sci-fi aesthetic", and echoes *Green Lantern: Earth One*. The production designer was John Paino ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanterns_(TV_series))). Kyle Chandler said three final suit designs went to Mundy and director James Hawes to choose from ([ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/?p=2177167)). The suit sets a rule the ring follows: on Hal's battered chest plate, "the Green Lantern symbol still shines clean" ([FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/lanterns-reveals-first-look-at-hal-jordans-battered-green-lantern-suit/)).

The ring's own build is undocumented. Apart from Johnson's one-line reply, **no designer, sculptor, jeweller or fabricator is named**. Materials, number of copies and weathering method are unknown. Neither the San Diego Comic-Con (SDCC) 2026 Hall H panel nor the teaser credits mention ring props ([tbreak](https://tbreak.com/lanterns-sdcc-make-detective-story-wonderfully/); [Animation World Network (AWN)](https://www.awn.com/print/news/hbo-max-releases-lanterns-teaser)). Brass comes up only for aftermarket rings that posters on The Replica Prop Forum (RPF) say have "no lineage" to the screen prop ([RPF](https://www.therpf.com/forums/threads/new-lanterns-ring.361843/post-5741186)). The effects were digital work by about **ten visual effects (VFX) studios**, including Digital Domain, Image Engine and Spin VFX, under supervisor Greg Steele ([SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/?p=688722)). The vendor list also includes Halon and SCANable ([AWN](https://www.awn.com/print/news/hbo-max-releases-lanterns-teaser)). Editor Vikash Patel described a "very crude bird" temporary effect that was refined in post ([Motion Picture Association (MPA)](https://www.motionpictures.org/2026/09/lanterns-editor-vikash-patel-on-pace-performance-power-rings/)). **Whether the ring itself had light-emitting diodes (LEDs), on-set lighting or only compositing is not reported.** No *Lanterns*-branded replica exists. McFarlane's DC Direct Hal Jordan ring statue is based on the comics ([Entertainment Earth](https://www.entertainmentearth.com/product/dc-direct-green-lantern-hal-jordan-power-ring-resin-statue/mf17944)). The only show-specific rings were promotional: custom rings given away at SDCC in fans' chosen sizes ([SDCC Blog](https://sdccblog.com/2026/07/review-get-recruited-into-the-world-of-lanterns-at-san-diego-comic-con-2026/)) and an oversize ring at the centre of the premiere's red carpet ([BizBash](https://www.bizbash.com/experiential-marketing/how-the-gritty-world-of-hbos-lanterns-was-turned-into-a-glam-hollywood-event)). Neither is described in detail.

## Sixty-seven years of rings kept one layout and changed the face

Every version keeps a **band plus one dominant green face carrying a circle-based emblem**, and in none of them does the band glow. Gil Kane's 1959 original is "a simple ring band", "the same shape/width all the way 'round", topped by a disc "emblazoned with the GL symbol". Later artists stuck a **3-D emblem on top of the band**, sometimes with a flush or domed gem, while "chunk" and Honor Guard styles blended the symbol into a ribbed band ([RPF](https://www.therpf.com/forums/threads/defined-green-lantern-comic-rings.119993/page-2)). The emblem itself kept changing. The 1982 DC Style Guide fixed sidebars "thinner than the central ring", Kyle Rayner's 1994 ring brought in a yin-yang-like mark, and *Rebirth* (2004) slanted the sidebars into the circle ([RPF](https://www.therpf.com/forums/threads/defined-green-lantern-comic-rings.119993/post-5101731)).

The **2011 film** turned this into a physical artifact: a distressed metal band holding a **translucent green faux crystal**. The replica was cast from the screen ring and does not light up ([DC.com](https://www.dc.com/blog/2011/02/10/green-lantern-dc-direct-movie-products-unveiled)). The energy was computer-generated (CG) and modelled on deep-sea creatures with heavy subsurface scattering ([Animation Magazine](https://animationmagazine.net/2011/06/seeing-the-green-light)). **Justice League** (2017, and the 2021 Snyder cut) shows a ring only briefly, when Yalan Gur's ring "zips off" after his death, and no design detail is published ([The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/justice-league-snyder-cut-green-lantern-easter-eggs-kilowog)). ***Superman* (2025)** brought the 2011 look back on purpose. Its prop master had worked on the 2011 film, and the new ring "looks nearly identical", except that the metal beside the emblem runs fully up to it instead of tapering to points ([The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-studios-green-lantern-ryan-reynolds-ring-dcu-reboot); [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/movies/feature/a-notorious-dc-flop-directly-inspired-superman-in-a-way-nobody-noticed/)). One writer called Guy's ring "entirely green in that crystalline way" ([Sandler's Handlings](https://sandlershandlings.substack.com/p/max-comes-bearing-first-lanterns-photo-to-mark-production-start)). The best reconciliation is a vivid crystal emblem on darker worn metal.

Against that history, *Lanterns* changes three things. It **breaks up the all-green face with metal trim**: gold for Hal, silver for John ([The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-james-gunn-dcu-ring)). It **desaturates the green**. And it uses **wear to mark status** between rings that share a layout. The face shape barely changes. This means the DC Universe (DCU) now has visually different rings for Guy and Hal even though, in the story, the ring passes between them. TVLine flags the mismatch, and no source explains it ([TVLine](https://www.tvline.com/2238858/green-lantern-hal-jordan-ring-lanterns-explained-guy-gardner/)).

## What a Minecraft ring should borrow, and what it should leave behind

The traits worth taking are structural, and none needs the circle-between-bars emblem. **Silhouette first:** "an icon should be easy to distinguish from all others by its shape alone" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/icon-design-rules-you-should-know)). The face should be clearly wider and taller than the band so the item never reads as a plain hoop at 16×16 pixels. As a working ratio (my suggestion, not a screen measurement), a band 2 pixels thick with a face 5 to 6 pixels across, about 2.5 to 3 times the band, matches the chunky signet look. **Value split:** a dark, unlit band against a bright face covers the dark, medium and light ranges Microsoft recommends, with at least half the icon at a 3.0:1 contrast ratio ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/WINDOWS/APPS/design/iconography/app-icon-design)). **One focal mark, with detail only on the top layer:** an original emblem of one or two bold shapes, such as a ring-in-ring, a single bar or a notched circle, gives the same instant read without copying (same sources).

The show adds four more ideas, each of which fits this mod's design. **Light only the face**: make the gem the only emissive or translucent element and keep the band a dark frame. That matches every screen ring and lets the mod's `CorpsTint` recolour the gem by corps without touching the metal. **Layer the face**: John's concentric dot, ring and trim stack shows how three nested circles suggest a gem with depth using very few pixels ([The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring)). **Make condition and trim the variant axis**: cracks against a clean finish and warm trim against cool trim set Hal's ring apart from the rookie ring without a new shape ([SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/news/654056-lanterns-teaser-green-lantern-ring-third-john-stewart)). That is a texture swap on one model. **Keep the emblem clean on a scuffed body**, following the suit's "symbol still shines clean" rule. On a 3D hand model, keep the face thin. Prop makers warn the signet look gets "more ridiculous the thicker they make the face" ([RPF](https://www.therpf.com/forums/threads/defined-green-lantern-comic-rings.119993/page-2)), and Microsoft advises avoiding "extremes of thick and thin shapes".

Avoid the show's emblem, its exact gold-outlined layout and any "Green Lantern" mark, since those are the protected parts. Avoid neon or lime green. Summers deliberately stayed away from greens that would be "shouting at us" ([SYFY Wire](https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/why-lanterns-broke-tradition-with-green-lantern-hal-jordan-suit-exclusive)). A slightly deep jade gem stays on-brief and leaves room to brighten it when the ring fires.

## Conclusion

The show's distinctive move is to treat **condition as identity**. One signet-and-gem layout becomes a relic or a rookie ring through cracks, finish and trim metal alone. For a mod whose rings already change colour by corps, this splits the parts cleanly: silhouette and value pattern make it read as a power ring, the gem's tint shows the corps, and wear or trim can show history or rank. The show's spare use of glow also fits a charge-limited power system. A gem that dims as charge runs down, and flares only on big powers, is the same 5%-battery logic the show uses in its story.

The evidence has a ceiling. Every physical description is secondhand, drawn from press reads of stills plus one prop-master reply. Finger, scale, materials and how the glow was made are all unknown. That suits an original design. The show offers principles (a dark worn band, a bright layered face, a clean emblem on a scuffed body, rationed light) rather than a blueprint, and the emblem and exact proportions are the parts a modeller should invent.
