# Fallen Titan room bosses: original pitch (2026-10-19)

J, after the Mutant Monsters rooms page: "I need something original", then "Mix, you pitch". These six bosses
replace the Mutant Monsters idea for the Fallen Titan (`2026-10-19-trials-concept.md`, "★ 1. The Fallen Titan").
They mix three sources:

- **Construct:** the Titan's own broken constructs waking up, built from its stone and light.
- **Fallen Lantern:** ghosts of other corps' Lanterns who died in the Titan (Red rage, Orange greed).
- **Parasite:** things that nested in the dead giant.

What we kept from the Mutant Monsters research (`docs/research/2026-10-19-mutant-monsters-bosses.md`): a good ring
fight needs constructs, not just Beam, and our construct blocks are unbreakable and explosion-proof (strength -1,
blast resistance 3,600,000). Every boss below is built around that. Yellow stays out of the rooms: the head and the
pilot finale are still the yellow fear storm.

Health is in hit points (2 = one heart). The player has 20.

Picture page: https://claude.ai/artifact/3bCtijyZohEWHMqp1WHn9d. Pictures are quick drawn concepts, not models.

## 1. The Sole Leech (Parasite) · the boot

- **Look:** a 14-block mud-skinned lamprey-ray living in the flooded boot. Its back is studded with pale-green pores
  where it drinks the light leaking from the Titan's sole. Its round mouth is ringed with teeth of stolen lantern
  glass, and swallowed green light glows in its throat. Wet mud hide, glass teeth; only the pores (light 7) and throat
  (light 12) glow.
- **Size:** 14 long, 3 tall, 4 wide. **Health:** 180.
- **Attacks:**
  - **Mud Drag:** surfaces under you and drags you 6 blocks into the mud: 8 damage, Slowness II for 3 s.
  - **Light Drink:** within 5 blocks of its mouth it drains 10% ring charge per second and heals 2 for each 10%.
  - **Geyser Lash:** its tail bursts out of another mud pool: 10 damage in a 3-block line, throws you 1.5 up.
- **Ring counter:** it can only move and surface through mud, and it can't be hurt while under (90% of the time). The
  floor has 8 mud pools. Lay Bridge or Wall flat to pave a pool shut (construct blocks can't be broken). With one pool
  left it has to surface there and beaches for 6 s, taking double damage. A Dome over the last pool traps it on top.
- **Why it's original:** the arena is the boss's sea, and you win by shrinking it with hard light. A thing that eats
  light against a weapon made of light.

## 2. The Hollow Rager (Fallen Lantern, Red) · the leg

- **Look:** the burned-out husk of a Red Lantern who died climbing the Titan to take it. It's jammed in the knee at the
  top of the hollow shin, two claw arms dug into the walls. Charred red-black armour, a cracked ruby heart (light 15)
  pouring rage plasma down the shaft like a red waterfall (light 10). Our three-slash claw mark is burned into the
  walls (original mark, no corps logos).
- **Size:** 5 tall, 9 arm span. **Health:** 220.
- **Attacks:**
  - **Rage Torrent:** a 3-wide column of plasma down the whole shaft: 6 per second while you're in it, and 4 s of fire.
    It shifts left or right every 8 s.
  - **Drop Claw:** lets go of one wall and drops 12 blocks onto you: 14 damage and a 4-block shockwave. It takes 5 s to
    climb back up (your window).
  - **Spite:** fills each time you hit it without being hit. When full it screams (Nausea 4 s) and doubles the torrent
    width for 6 s.
- **Ring counter:** the torrent pools on any construct. Climb by building Walls as umbrella ledges. When 3 blocks of
  plasma have pooled on a Wall, Shockwave the Wall upward: the pooled rage flies back up at him for 30 damage. That's
  the only thing that breaks his claw grip: 3 returns and he falls to the shin floor, stunned 8 s. Beam into the
  torrent is eaten (25% damage).
- **Why it's original:** a boss fought from below, beaten with its own attack, using your construct as a bucket.

## 3. The False Heart (Parasite + Construct) · the chest cavity

- **Look:** a 9-block lump of crystal-flesh hanging from the ribs on 6 veins, beating with a sickly yellow-green light
  it pumps into the Titan. Where its veins touch the stone, broken construct parts twitch awake: the rib arms. Across
  the cavity the real lantern-heart socket sits dark.
- **Size:** 9 across; rib arms 12 long. **Health:** 300 (and 3 rib arms of 60).
- **Attacks:**
  - **Systole:** one beat every 4 s (every 2 s under half health): 10 damage within 6 blocks, pushes you 4 out.
  - **Rib Sweep:** a woken stone rib swings across the cavity: 12 damage, knocks you off ledges.
  - **Vein Lash:** a vein whips 12 blocks out and pulls you 5 blocks toward the heart, Poison 3 s.
- **Ring counter:** it heals 5 per beat through each live vein, so Beam alone never wins. Build a Bridge from each
  vein's rib anchor across to the real socket: the construct reroutes the light, that vein goes dark and the real
  heart glows a little greener. All 6 bypassed and it starves (triple damage) and the rib arms go still. Finish it
  inside a Dome and it collapses; the real heart lights and the Titan's chest glows green.
- **Why it's original:** heart surgery as a boss fight. Your constructs are the bypass vessels, and winning re-lights
  the Titan, which is the trial's repair twist.

## 4. The Tollkeeper (Fallen Lantern, Orange) · the arm

- **Look:** the hunched ghost of an Orange Lantern in a long coat of overlapping coin plates, two orange eyes in a
  hood, one arm far too long. He sits on the far side of the broken arm on a hoard of stolen constructs, every one
  turned orange (light 10).
- **Size:** 4.5 tall, arm reaches 10 blocks. **Health:** 200.
- **Attacks:**
  - **Covet:** every 6 s he takes the biggest construct you have within 10 blocks (bridge pieces too). It turns orange
    and joins his hoard.
  - **Coin Hail:** 8 hard-light coins arc down at you, 3 each.
  - **Hoarded Wall:** throws a hoard construct back: 9 damage, and it stands as an orange wall in your path for 5 s.
- **Ring counter:** you need constructs to cross the gap, and he steals them. He always grabs the biggest one, so build
  a big bait (a Dome or wide Wall) away from the bridge and dissolve it the instant his hand closes: he grabs nothing
  and his arm hangs out stunned for 3 s (double damage). His life is in the hoard: Lasso a hoard pile off the arm into
  the valley for -40 health each. While the hoard is full, he hides behind it from Beam.
- **Why it's original:** a boss that wants your constructs. The fight is bait and timing your dissolve.

## 5. The Rootwyrm (Parasite) · extra room: the spine

- **Look:** a 30-block centipede of bark and root, moss on its back and spore lights on every third segment. Its legs
  are roots that plunge through the Titan's back into the valley floor. It's why the Titan never got up: it has
  stitched the giant to the ground. Fought outside, on the Titan's back under the night sky, among the trees growing
  out of it.
- **Size:** 30 long, 3 thick. **Health:** 260 (and 6 anchor roots of 40).
- **Attacks:**
  - **Root Spear:** roots burst up through the back plates under you: 10 damage, after 1 s of cracking.
  - **Spore Bloom:** a 5-block cloud: Slowness II and Weakness for 5 s, and no flight while you're in it.
  - **Coil:** wraps you, 4 per second for up to 3 s; Shockwave breaks out.
- **Ring counter:** it regrows wherever you aren't. Jam a Wall under an anchor root as a wedge (it can't pierce
  construct), then Tether the root out of the valley floor. Each root out, the Titan shifts; all 6 out and the Titan
  rolls 10 degrees and the wyrm slides off its back into the valley, where Beam finishes it.
- **Why it's original:** the fight un-pins a giant, and winning moves the whole level. It gives the Titan's stand-up a
  reason.

## 6. The Last Order (Construct) · extra room: the hand

- **Look:** a 10-block knight built from cracked grey Titan plating, its hard-light seams dimmed to a failing green,
  a visor slit and a grey construct hammer (J's 843 MAa mech-hammer idea). It's still following its dead owner's last
  order: hold the line. Three cracks are open on its body. Fought on the open palm, between the finger pillars.
- **Size:** 10 tall. **Health:** 250; at 0 it kneels for 10 s, then stands again at 100.
- **Attacks:**
  - **Line Hammer:** overhead slam: 16 damage and a 4-block shockwave along the palm.
  - **Shard Volley:** 6 plating shards, 4 each.
  - **Overwrite:** a grey pulse that dissolves every construct you have within 6 blocks, every 15 s (the visor flares
    first).
- **Ring counter:** it's a broken ally, not an enemy. While it kneels (or after a Shockwave stun), press a Wall or Bridge
  into a crack to patch it: that seam turns bright green and Overwrite stops hitting your constructs. All 3 patched
  and it recognises your ring, kneels and joins you; in the pilot finale it rides the Titan's shoulder and fights the
  storm with you. Brute force (3 knock-downs) shatters it, and you get no ally.
- **Why it's original:** a boss you mend instead of kill. The repair theme becomes a character.

## Round 3: one boss through the whole Titan (2026-10-19)

J: "One boss, whole Titan ★" (over the Titan's own machines, giant vanilla mobs, or describing one). The thing that
killed the Titan still lives inside it and hunts you room to room, the way the spider lair works. Three takes, each
rendered as a hero shot beside the player and inside the whole Titan cut open from above
(`tools/trials/titan_killer_blend.py`, `LanternLive\trials\titan_killer.blend`; the Titan block-out is a 60-block
hollow construct lying face-down, one boot sunk in mud, the right arm snapped):

- **K1 the Gnawer:** an 80-block fear centipede threaded through the whole body (boot, leg, pelvis, chest, out along
  the arm), black chitin with yellow fear bands and legs. You see parts of it in every room; its head hunts you and it
  bursts through the walls between rooms.
- **K2 the Hollow Lantern:** a gaunt Yellow Lantern wraith (about 12 blocks) who killed the Titan's pilot: tattered
  cloak, bone arms, a caged lantern for a skull, fear-blade constructs circling it. It stalks you from room to room.
- **K3 the Fear Seed:** a hunched giant made of hundreds of yellow construct cubes in the chest, with streams of cubes
  running down every limb: the fear storm growing inside the corpse. It reforms in whichever room you're in.
