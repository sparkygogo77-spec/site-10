## Lessons for the design skill

- Standing take-off settles low: "Just 2.2" blocks of hover, never above the snap height (403 → 407).
- Landings carry almost nothing: "0.3 carry blocks" on L4, under 0.3 s (405).
- Descend and the double-tap landing must work while moving forward, not only standing: "when im moving forward i cant
  descend like when im standing still" (405).
- Judge arm poses on the body the game uses at that speed: slow flight is upright, the sprint is a flat glide; the
  "above the head" arm in 404 was the glide (404 → 406).
- The free arm sits where a player's arm is: "angled about where the player's arm is"; A2 slightly forward is the dart's
  free arm, A1 the option, A3 dropped (403).
- Take-offs are tiny and never hold you: "maybe the take off should be a very small animation", "allow me to fly once
  im flying" (401/402).
- First person only when the move has one: "i shouldnt have 1st person renders for a third person animation".
- Review happens on the site, rated KEEP / IMPROVE / DROP; an IMPROVE with no note means ask him, and he may answer
  "Drop it" (R3A3).

## Gotchas

- Cloud rendering is too slow (2 cores, about 1.5 s a frame): render on J's PC with EEVEE.
- Blender 5.2 FFMPEG output: set `image_settings.media_type = "VIDEO"` before choosing FFMPEG, or the enum errors.
- A fresh clone on J's PC has no git identity: the renderer's `ensure_identity()` sets it.
- A new PowerShell window starts in the wrong folder: `cd C:\Users\Josh\Documents\LanternLive` first, or it fails with
  OSError.
- Specs that use assets not in the blend (construct_sword bbmodel, suit_tex strips, tool_tex) need them seeded from
  `review-site\assets` and the LanternLive folders into `_review_build`.
- After pulling a renderer update, the running Python keeps the old code: the renderer restarts itself via runpy with
  `LR_RELOADED`.
- Every push to `review-site` is a Vercel deploy (85 in a day once): push once per run; every other branch keeps its
  `vercel.json` with `deploymentEnabled: false`. Never push to `review-site` from a chat.
- `pkill -f` on a pattern can kill your own shell: match the exact process.
- Another chat broke the INBOX template once: check the top of INBOX.md after a rebase.
- The first `device_list_dir` with `recursive: true` on LanternLive passes 2,000 entries (it's truncated, and the
  result is too big for context): list the area folders one by one instead.
