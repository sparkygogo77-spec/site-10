# Lanterns (HBO, 2026) power ring: on-screen design

Research as of 2026-10-13. All sources are secondary entertainment press. I found no official HBO, DC Studios or prop-department design write-up describing the ring in detail. Words only; no images reproduced.

## Shape and proportions (band, face/bezel, size, setting)

### Takeaway
Hal Jordan's ring is a thick, dark, chunky band with a rough stone-like texture and cracks. Its face is a round emblem with a raised-looking gold outline around a green gem. No prongs or open setting are described anywhere. No source gives exact proportions such as band width or face diameter.

### Cited Findings
- Hal's ring (first official photo): the band is "thick and dark," with a "rock-like" texture and cracks running down its length, and looks thousands of years old. It carries the classic Green Lantern symbol outlined in gold with a green gem at its centre. The article is dated 2026-01-08 and credits the image to DC Studios — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-james-gunn-dcu-ring)
- Same description in a later piece: the classic emblem with a gold outline, a green gem, and a band that is "dark and cracked." The look is called "ancient, worn" by fans who like it and "rough, gold-accented" by fans who don't (2026-02-10) — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-2026-hal-jordan-john-stewart-dcu-rings)
- Hal's ring was first shown officially around 2025-12-12 — [ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/?p=2103803); [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/news/654056-lanterns-teaser-green-lantern-ring-third-john-stewart)
- John's ring, from Episode 3 "OutKast" stills plus a fan analysis: a black band with silver trim. The face is concentric: a small black centre circle, then a green ring, then another ring, then the silver trim. Hal's face is instead one larger green circle with no black centre (2026-09-27) — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring)
- An April official look at a Lanterns ring describes a "more crystallized aesthetic" with "a series of concentric rings at its center" (2026-04-14) — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-ring-dc-live-action)

### Inferences
- Both rings look like chunky signet-style rings with a flat-topped round face, not a cut stone held in prongs. This is inferred from "band plus outlined emblem plus centre gem" and from no source mentioning prongs.

### Gaps
- I found no measurements, no side-profile description, and no statement of how far the ring stands off the finger or which finger it is worn on.

## Materials and finish (metal, stone/glass, wear, engraving)

### Takeaway
Hal's ring has a dark, stone-like or blackened band that is cracked and aged, a gold-coloured outline on the emblem, and a green gem or crystal. John's looks newer and cleaner, with silver trim instead of gold. The show deliberately gave them different wear.

### Cited Findings
- Hal's ring is not fully green, unlike Guy Gardner's ring in *Superman* (2025). The gold outline is a new element. Some fans wanted a "machined finish" instead of the rough, unfinished look (2026-01-08) — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-james-gunn-dcu-ring)
- The teaser (2026-03-05) shows Hal's ring as worn, "suggesting years of battles," and John's as looking newer — [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/news/654056-lanterns-teaser-green-lantern-ring-third-john-stewart); [ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/?p=2103803)
- John's ring has silver trim and Hal's has gold. Prop master Jeffrey Johnson replied "Indeed he did" on the fan comparison post, confirming the rings differ (2026-09-27) — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring)
- The ring prop worn by Kyle Chandler and Aaron Pierre differs from Nathan Fillion's *Superman* ring, which the article puts down to aesthetic choices (2026-09-20) — [Nerdist](https://nerdist.com/article/why-doesnt-guy-gardner-have-hal-jordan-ring-lanterns/)

### Inferences
- The "crystallized" centre suggests the green part is a translucent gem or crystal, not paint or enamel.

### Gaps
- I found no confirmation of the actual prop materials (metal, resin or 3D print), and no source mentions engraving or inscriptions.

## The emblem

### Takeaway
The lantern emblem is a round face. On Hal's ring the outline is gold and the centre is a green gem. On John's the outline is silver and the face is a set of concentric rings around a black centre.

### Cited Findings
- Hal's ring shows "the classic Green Lantern symbol, outlined in gold, with a green gem at its center" — [The Direct, 2026-01-08](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-james-gunn-dcu-ring)
- John's face: a black centre dot, a green ring, another ring, then silver trim (fan read of official stills, partly endorsed by the prop master) — [The Direct, 2026-09-27](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring)
- **Conflict:** the 2026-04-14 Direct piece says John's ring has a *golden* outline and a green centre gem — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-ring-dc-live-action). The later 2026-09-27 piece, based on episode stills, says silver trim — [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring). The later account rests on more footage and on the prop master's comment.

### Inferences
- The emblem seems to be a coloured gem with a metal outline around it, so it reads as inset or raised rather than engraved. No source says the emblem glows at rest.

### Gaps
- I found no description of the emblem lighting up on the ring itself, as opposed to the energy effects around it.

## Colour and glow / VFX

### Takeaway
The show keeps green-light effects deliberately sparse and grounded. Big glowing green constructs appear only a few times. Hal's full-power moment in Episode 1 is shielding or containing a blast. Charging happens by pressing the ring against an alien-looking green power battery.

### Cited Findings
- Episode 1 uses "gigantic, glowy green energy constructs" sparingly. Most absurd feats are only mentioned in dialogue. Small ring tricks shown include a bottle opener and fake money. Flight is "unceremonious." Hal uses full power once, to contain an alien self-destruct device. Overall the effects are a restrained contrast to the "garish" 2011 film (2026-08-19) — [Looper](https://www.looper.com/2240128/lanterns-avoid-worst-mistake-green-lantern-movie/)
- In Episode 1, Hal shields Rushville from a neutron bomb and is left with almost no charge. The ring's energy is finite and depends on concentration (2026-08-19) — [JustWatch (German)](https://guides.justwatch.com/de/lanterns-green-lantern-ring-schwaeche-erklaert)
- Promo still on HBO Max Instagram: a fist presses the ring against a "strange, alien-looking" battery that is plainly green, against a dark, drab setting (2026-04-30) — [Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/tv/lanterns-promo-image-has-actual-green-in-it/)
- VFX: ten studios worked on the show, including Digital Domain, Image Engine and Spin VFX. Greg Steele was production VFX supervisor and Eddie Bonin VFX producer. Constructs were planned for and were not the biggest challenge (2026-08-25) — [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/?p=688722)
- Earlier rings for comparison: Guy's *Superman* ring is all green and its jewel glows when activated. The 2011 ring had a sleek metallic band and constant bright green energy — [The Direct, 2026-04-14](https://thedirect.com/article/green-lantern-ring-dc-live-action)

### Inferences
- The green has not been named precisely, but promo stills use it as the only saturated colour against muted, desaturated grading.

### Gaps
- I found no frame-by-frame description of the ring glowing, the exact green shade, or particle or flare treatment, and no VFX-house breakdown specific to the ring.

## Hal vs John, and other rings

### Takeaway
The rings are confirmed different. Hal's is old and cracked with gold trim and a single green face. John's is newer with silver trim and a concentric face around a black centre. Guy's *Superman* ring is a third design. No source describes a Sinestro yellow ring on screen.

### Cited Findings
- Differences: gold vs silver trim, and a single green circle vs a concentric face with a black centre. Prop master Jeffrey Johnson confirmed the rings differ — [The Direct, 2026-09-27](https://thedirect.com/article/dc-lanterns-john-stewart-green-lantern-ring)
- Hal's ring is worn and John's is newer, which fits the gap in their experience — [SuperHeroHype, 2026-03-05](https://www.superherohype.com/news/654056-lanterns-teaser-green-lantern-ring-third-john-stewart)
- Plot: Hal is murdered and his ring is decommissioned by the Guardians. Guy gets a newly forged ring in Episode 6, "Bad Optics" — [Nerdist, 2026-09-20](https://nerdist.com/article/why-doesnt-guy-gardner-have-hal-jordan-ring-lanterns/)
- Sinestro (Ulrich Thomsen) debuts in Episode 2. The coverage describes only the comics' yellow fear ring and does not confirm that he wears one on screen — [Looper, 2026-08-23](https://www.looper.com/2240373/lanterns-episode-2-ulrich-thomsen-sinestro-debut-explained/); [Nerdtropolis, SDCC trailer, 2026-07-24](https://nerdtropolis.com/lanterns-sdcc-trailer-reveals-sinestro-shape-shifting-manhunters-and-returning-heroes/)
- Finale (Episode 8, "Dirt and Stars"): Sinestro forges a lantern with yellow streaks in its casing, infused with fear toxin. John uses it to charge his own ring and calls himself an "independent contractor" — [/Film, 2026-10-05](https://www.slashfilm.com/2278209/lanterns-finale-sinestro-corps-setup-explained/)

### Inferences
- A yellow Sinestro Corps ring looks set up for season 2 rather than shown in season 1. This is speculative.

### Gaps
- I found no description of what John's ring looks like after the yellow-lantern charge, and no source on a visible yellow ring for Sinestro.
